    Roadmap to the platinum

    Six stages, in order. Work them top to bottom: the story first, then the 100% checklist, then side strands, collectibles, activities and the final grinds. The counts update as you tick trophies off.

      Missables & warnings

      Before you start

      • Only one trophy is reported as potentially missable — see above. Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story.
      • There is no mission replay. The game has 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave slot: keep a save at the start and end of each chapter, then rotate the remaining slots for free-roam cleanup.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Keep cheats off on your trophy run.
      • No online trophies. All 55 trophies are single-player and offline — no multiplayer, no DLC and no online mode is needed for the platinum.
      • There are no difficulty settings and no New Game+, so one playthrough is enough.
